## The problem this solves

Your GoHighLevel account knows a lead came in, an opportunity moved to Booked, an order was paid. Meta and Google know none of it. They optimize on what gets reported back to them, so if all they see is a landing-page form submit, that's what they buy you more of.

Hello Conversions watches the events in your Lead Connector (GoHighLevel) account, decides which ones count as conversions, and delivers them to Meta's Conversions API and Google Ads offline conversions with click IDs and match data attached. Then it reports on the result, so ad spend and CRM revenue sit in one view.

## Who it's for

Agency operators running client accounts — one project per client, separate integrations, destinations and numbers, under one organization and one bill. It works the same way for a single business.

You'll need:

- A GoHighLevel account you can authorize (a location, not just a sub-account login)
- At least one ad account on Meta or Google running spend
- Pipelines, tags, or orders that mean something — events are only as good as the CRM hygiene behind them

## What you'll set up

A **project** for the account, a **source** to pull data in, a **destination** to push conversions out, and a **connection** that says which events go where. Everything else sits on top.
