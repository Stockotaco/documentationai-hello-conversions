## The problem this solves

Your GoHighLevel account already knows what happened. A lead came in, an opportunity moved to Booked, an order was paid. That is the outcome you actually care about, and it lives in the CRM.

Meta and Google don't know any of it. They optimize on what gets reported back to them, so if all they ever see is a form submit on a landing page, that is what they buy you more of — form submits, not booked calls and not paid orders.

Hello Conversions is the pipe between the two. It watches the events in your Lead Connector (GoHighLevel) account, decides which ones count as conversions, and delivers them to Meta's Conversions API and Google Ads offline conversions with the click IDs and match data attached. Then it reports on the result, so ad spend and CRM revenue sit in the same view.

## Who it's for

It's built for agency operators running client accounts — one project per client, separate integrations, separate destinations, separate numbers, all under one organization and one bill. It works exactly the same way with a single project if you run one business rather than a book of them.

You'll get the most out of it if you already have:

- A GoHighLevel account you can authorize (a location, not just a sub-account login)
- At least one ad account on Meta or Google running spend
- Pipelines, tags, or orders in GoHighLevel that mean something — the events you send are only as good as the CRM hygiene behind them

## What you'll set up

Four things, in this order: a **project** for the account, a **source** to pull data in, a **destination** to push conversions out, and a **connection** that says which events go where. That's the whole model. Everything else — filters, pipeline stage automations, reporting — sits on top of it.
