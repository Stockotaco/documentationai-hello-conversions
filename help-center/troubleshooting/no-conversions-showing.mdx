---
title: No Conversions Showing
description: Fix when conversions aren’t tracked.
---

## Symptoms
- Conversion events are missing in dashboard
- No data on ad platforms

## Causes
- Script not installed
- Pixel blocked by browser

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check script installation">
    ````html
    Ensure the Hello Conversions script is added before </head> in every funnel page.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache or use incognito">
    Cached pages may run old scripts; try incognito mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-test conversion">
    Fill out your funnel form again to verify tracking.
  </Step>
</Steps>
