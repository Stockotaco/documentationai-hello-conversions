---
title: No Conversions Showing
description: Fix when conversions aren’t tracked.
---

## Symptoms
- Conversion events are missing in dashboard
- No data on ad platforms

## Causes
- A source or destination isn't connected
- No connection is routing the event to a destination
- The event doesn't match a connection's filters

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your sources and destinations">
    Under **Settings → Integrations**, confirm the project has a source (e.g. GoHighLevel) and a destination (Meta or Google Ads) connected, and that each shows a healthy status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your connections">
    A conversion only reaches an ad platform when a connection routes its event type to a destination. Open the connection list and confirm one covers the event you expect — and that it's enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Activity Log">
    Open the [Activity Log](/conversion-tracking/activity-history) and submit a test form. The event should appear with its delivery outcome; if it was filtered or failed, the log says why.
  </Step>
</Steps>
