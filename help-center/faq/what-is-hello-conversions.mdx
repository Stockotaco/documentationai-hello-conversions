## Overview

Hello Conversions helps GHL users fix ad tracking issues and see every conversion, fast.

### Key points:

- Simple tracking for GoHighLevel (GHL) funnels
- Works with Facebook, Google and TikTok ads
- Tracks form submissions and appointments
- Copy-paste install: works in 90 seconds

## Why use Hello Conversions?

- Capture every conversion, no missed data
- Works even if GHL native tracking fails
- Simple dashboard for viewing results
