## Overview

Hello Conversions watches what happens in your GoHighLevel account — orders, pipeline stages, tags, new contacts — and reports those conversions back to Meta and Google Ads server-side, with click IDs and identity data attached. Then it puts your ad spend and your CRM revenue in one set of reports.

### Key points:

- Connects to GoHighLevel (Lead Connector) as the source of every conversion event
- Delivers conversions to Meta's Conversions API and Google Ads offline conversion upload
- Pulls ad spend in from Meta Ads and Google Ads so cost sits next to revenue
- Reports on revenue, lead value over time, marketing efficiency, creatives and delivery health

## Who it's for

Agency operators running client accounts — one project per client, separate integrations and numbers, under one organization and one bill. It works the same way for a single business.

## What you need

- A GoHighLevel account you can authorize (a location, not just a sub-account login)
- At least one ad account on Meta or Google running spend
- Pipelines, tags or orders that mean something — conversions are only as good as the CRM hygiene behind them
