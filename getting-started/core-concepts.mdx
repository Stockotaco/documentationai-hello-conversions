title: Core Concepts
description: The seven objects Hello Conversions is built from — organization, project, source, destination, connection, pipeline, and contact — and how they fit together.
---

An **organization** holds **projects**. A project pulls data in from **sources** and pushes conversions out to **destinations**. A **connection** says which events go to which destination. **Pipelines** build those rules from your CRM stages. **Contacts** are the people it's all about.

## Organization

Owns your projects, your teammates, and your bill — the plan, subscription status, trial, and the contact count that sets your price. New projects inherit the plan.

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, and the only role that can see or change billing. |
| **Member** | Works in the projects they've been given access to. |

An invite grants access to every project or only specific ones. Members can also auto-join new projects.

<Callout kind="note">
  Billing is owner-only and enforced on the server, not just hidden in the sidebar. A member who navigates straight to a billing URL gets a permission error, not a page.
</Callout>

## Project

One client account — or just your business, if you're not an agency. The unit of isolation: its own sources, destinations, connections, pipeline configuration, tracking script, timezone and reporting. Data never crosses between projects, and project membership is separate from organization membership.

## Source

**A source is data coming in**, managed under **Settings → Integrations → Sources**.

| Group | Sources | What it brings in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM & Revenue | Lead Connector, Stripe | Contacts, pipelines, orders, webhook events, charges and subscriptions |
| Ad Platforms | Meta Ads, Google Ads | Campaign data, ad spend, performance metrics |
| Data Enrichment | PostHog | Session and product analytics attached to conversion data |

**Lead Connector is GoHighLevel**, the in-app name for the same thing, and everything else depends on it: it connects over OAuth to one location and streams the contact, opportunity, tag and order events that become conversions.

Ad platform and Stripe sources sync on a schedule, so a fresh one reads "Awaiting first sync" until its first run completes. Each project holds one connection per provider, with a live status: connected, error, or "Reconnect" when the provider revoked the token.

## Destination

**A destination is conversions going out**, configured under **Settings → Integrations → Destinations**.

| Destination | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Meta Conversions API | Sends server-side conversion events to a Meta pixel for ad optimization |
| Google Ads | Uploads offline conversions against a Google Ads customer account |
| Webhook | POSTs event payloads as JSON to any HTTP endpoint, optionally HMAC-signed |
| PostHog | Forwards events into PostHog for product analytics and session replay |

Each has its own name, credentials and delivery health — status, last error, last delivery. Several of the same type can coexist in one project, so name them: "Main Pixel", "Client Backup Pixel".

<Callout kind="alert">
  Meta Ads and Google Ads appear in both lists, and they are not the same setup. **Meta Ads the source** pulls your spend in. **Meta Conversions API the destination** pushes conversions out. A project with Meta Ads connected as a source but no Meta Conversions API destination is sending Meta nothing.
</Callout>

## Connection

The rule in the middle: **these source events go to that destination**. A connection selects one or more Lead Connector events, points them at exactly one destination, and can carry:

- **Filters** — only deliver on a match, such as an order above a value, one funnel, or one pipeline stage
- **A custom event name** — override what the destination is told the event is called
- **A conversion action** — for Google Ads, which conversion action the upload counts against
- **An enabled switch** — pause delivery without deleting the rule

They live under **Settings → Workflows → Connections**. One destination can be fed by many connections; one connection feeds exactly one destination, so reaching both Meta and Google means two.

## Pipeline

A GoHighLevel pipeline and its stages — Lead, Booked, Showed, Closed. **Settings → Workflows → Pipelines** reads them live from your connected location. Enable a stage, give it an event name (one is generated from the stage name), and choose its destinations. Saving generates one connection per enabled stage and destination, each filtered to that exact pipeline and stage.

<Callout kind="warning">
  These connections are regenerated from the pipeline config every time you save the Pipelines page, so edits made to them on the Connections page can be overwritten. Change pipeline-driven rules on the Pipelines page; use the Connections page for rules you build by hand.
</Callout>

## Contact and person

A **contact** is a person in your connected Lead Connector location — the CRM record with an email, phone, tags, opportunities and orders. Each project's count is snapshotted daily, and the organization-wide total sets your billing tier. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-billing).

A **person** is the visitor identity the tracking pixel builds from site activity, with its own timeline and attribution data — what lets an ad click and a CRM purchase be recognized as the same human.
