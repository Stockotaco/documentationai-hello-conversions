Billing sits on the **organization**, not on individual projects. One subscription covers every project in the organization, and adding a client project doesn't add a separate bill — it adds contacts, which is what you're actually charged on.

Everything described here lives under **Organization settings → Billing** and **Organization settings → Usage**.

<Callout kind="note">
  Only the organization owner can view or change billing. For members, the billing pages aren't just hidden in the navigation — the server refuses the request.
</Callout>

## The plans

| Plan | Who it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversions Plus+** | Getting conversions out of GoHighLevel and into the ad platforms. |
| **Reporting Suite** | Everything in Conversions Plus+, plus the analytics layer on top of it. |
| **Enterprise** | Organizations past the self-serve contact ceiling, or with custom requirements. |

Conversions Plus+ and Reporting Suite are the two you can subscribe to yourself, and you can move between them at any time from the billing page. Enterprise is arranged with us directly. An organization with no subscription sits on **Free**, which is not a usable tier so much as the state of not having subscribed yet.

The billing page shows the current feature list for each plan side by side, with your plan marked. Read it there rather than from a doc — it's the copy that ships with the app and it's what your subscription is actually described as.

## How pricing works

Both self-serve plans are priced on **contact tiers**, not a flat fee.

1. Every project's contact count — the number of contacts in its connected Lead Connector location — is snapshotted once a day.
2. Those counts are added up across all projects in the organization.
3. That total lands you in a contact tier, shown on the billing page as "Up to N contacts".
4. Your monthly price is the price of that tier on your plan. Reporting Suite costs more than Conversions Plus+ at every tier.

The tiers step up as you grow, so a single small client and an agency running twenty accounts pay different amounts on the same plan. The billing page shows your current tier, your current contact count against it, and the resulting monthly estimate for each plan — check there for current prices rather than anywhere else, since they're set centrally and can change.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Contact tiers have a self-serve ceiling of 100,000 contacts across the organization. Past it, checkout is blocked and you'll need enterprise pricing. The billing page warns you at 80% of that ceiling, which is the point to get in touch rather than the point you're stopped.
</Callout>

## Free trial

Your first subscription starts with a 7-day free trial. It's once per organization: if you've had a trial and later resubscribe, the new subscription starts billing immediately.

While you're on it, the billing page shows a countdown with the days remaining and the exact end date.

## What an active subscription gates

The gate is your **subscription status**, not your plan tier. These statuses keep the app open:

- **Trialing** — inside the free trial
- **Active** — paying normally
- **Canceling** — you've canceled, and the current period hasn't ended yet

Anything else — never subscribed, canceled and past the period end, or past due on payment — blocks access to the app with a notice pointing at billing. Billing pages themselves stay reachable in that state, on purpose, so you can fix it.

Nothing is deleted when a subscription lapses. Resubscribing restores access to your existing projects, connections and data.

## Managing the subscription

From **Organization settings → Billing** the owner can:

- **Subscribe** to Conversions Plus+ or Reporting Suite through hosted checkout
- **Switch plans** between the two, applied to the existing subscription
- **Open the billing portal** to update payment details and retrieve invoices
- **Cancel**, which cancels at the end of the current period — the status becomes "canceling" and access continues until the period ends
- **Review recent billing events**, listed on the same page with the plan and outcome of each

<Callout kind="info">
  Some organizations are billed outside the app — usually a subscription that predates self-serve checkout, or one arranged directly. Those show a notice on the billing page and can't use self-serve checkout or plan switching, because doing so would bill you twice. Contact support to change plans instead.
</Callout>

## Where to see usage

**Organization settings → Usage** is the detailed view, broken out per project for the current billing period:

- **Contacts** per project, as a trend line plus the latest figure
- **Events** per project — tracking pixel events, shown for visibility and not billed
- **Previous months**, expandable to see each month's totals and the per-project split behind them

Use it when the bill moves and you want to know which client account moved it. **Organization settings → Billing** carries the same contact total as a single meter against your current tier, which is the faster read when you just want to know how close you are to the next step up.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Contact counts come from the connected Lead Connector location. A project whose Lead Connector integration is disconnected stops reporting new counts and carries its last known figure forward, so a disconnection that goes unnoticed will quietly freeze that project's usage. The source status on **Settings → Integrations → Sources** is where you'd spot it.
</Callout>
