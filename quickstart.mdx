## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">

- An active GoHighLevel (`GHL`) account with admin access
- Connected ad accounts on Meta, Google Ads, or TikTok
- A web browser (Chrome recommended for best experience)

</Callout>

## Complete Setup in 90 Seconds

Follow these steps to connect your `GHL` account and see live conversion data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Your Free Trial" icon="user-plus">

    Visit the [pricing page](https://helloconversions.com/pricing) and click **Start 7-Day Free Trial**.

    Fill out the form with your email and create a password. No credit card required.

    After signup, you'll land on the [dashboard](https://cloud.helloconversions.com).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect GoHighLevel" icon="link">

    In the dashboard, navigate to **Integrations > GoHighLevel**.

    Copy your `GHL` API key from your `GHL` account settings (under **Settings > API Keys**).

    Paste the key into Hello Conversions and click **Connect**.

    <CodeGroup tabs="GHL API Key">

    ````bash
    # Example GHL API key format (yours will be longer)
    YOUR_GHL_API_KEY_HERE
    ````

    ````json
    {
      "api_key": "YOUR_GHL_API_KEY_HERE"
    }
    ````

    </CodeGroup>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Tracking Script" icon="code">

    Hello Conversions generates a tracking script. Copy it from **Setup > Tracking Code**.

    Paste the script into your `GHL` site's `<head>` section (via Custom Code or site builder).

    <CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,jQuery">

    ````javascript
    <!-- Hello Conversions Tracking Script -->
    <script>
      (function(h,e,l,l,o,c,o,n,s) {
        h[o] = h[o] || {q:[],c:function(){this.q.push(arguments)}};
        s = document.createElement('script');
        s.src = 'https://cloud.helloconversions.com/tracker.js';
        s.async = true;
        document.head.appendChild(s);
      })(
        window, document, 'HelloConversions', 'tracker', 'hcq'
      );
      hcq('init', 'YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID');
    </script>
    ````

    ````javascript
    // jQuery version (if preferred)
    $.getScript('https://cloud.helloconversions.com/tracker.js', function() {
      window.hcq('init', 'YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID');
    });
    ````

    </CodeGroup>

    Replace `YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID` with your dashboard account ID.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify First Conversions" icon="check-circle">

    Trigger a test form submission or purchase in your `GHL` funnel.

    Refresh the Hello Conversions dashboard. Events appear in **Real-Time Events** within seconds.

    Check **Daily Rev Ops** for revenue and conversion metrics.

    <Callout kind="success">

      Success! You now track every conversion, bypassing ad blockers and iOS limits.

    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Ad Platforms

Link your ad accounts for full attribution.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Meta Ads" icon="facebook">

    Go to **Integrations > Meta**.

    Authorize with your Meta Business Manager.

    Select campaigns to track conversions back to `GHL`.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Google Ads" icon="search">

    Navigate to **Integrations > Google**.

    Grant access via Google Ads account.

    Map conversions to `GHL` events.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
