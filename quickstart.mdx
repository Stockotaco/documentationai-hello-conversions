---
title: Quickstart
description: Create a project, connect GoHighLevel and an ad platform, add a destination, and confirm your first conversion was delivered.
---

About fifteen minutes. You need admin access to the GoHighLevel location you want to track, and to the ad account you're sending conversions to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project" icon="folder-plus">

    Click **New Project**, enter a **Website URL** (the domain you're tracking, like `example.com`) and an optional description, then **Create Project**.

    <Callout kind="note">
      The organization, not the project, carries the plan and the bill. If you see a subscription notice instead of your project, start at [Plans and billing](/account/plans-billing) — billing pages stay reachable even when the rest of the app is gated.
    </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Lead Connector" icon="link">

    Click **Get Started**, then **Connect** on **Lead Connector**, and approve it for the location you want to track. This is GoHighLevel, the source of every conversion event you'll send. Nothing else works without it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an ad platform" icon="megaphone">

    Connect **Meta Ads** (ad account ID, from Meta Business Suite → Ad Account Settings, like `act_123456789`) or **Google Ads** (customer ID, under the help icon top right, like `123-456-7890`), then authorize on the platform. Click **Complete Setup**.

    This brings spend and campaign data **in**, not conversions out — that's the next step, and separate setup even for the same platform.

    <Callout kind="info">
      Ad platform data syncs on a schedule. The source row reads "Awaiting first sync" until the first run finishes.
    </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a destination" icon="upload">

    At **Settings → Integrations → Destinations**, click **Add Destination** and pick a provider. Use **Test Connection** before saving.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Meta Conversions API">

        Paste the **Pixel ID** (Events Manager → Data Sources) and a **Conversions API access token** (Settings → Conversions API). Optionally set a **Website URL**, used as the event source URL when an event carries none, and a **Default Currency** for events with no currency of their own.

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Google Ads">

        Enter only your **Customer ID**, then send a linking request from the destination card and approve it inside Google Ads.

        <Callout kind="warning">
          Until that manager account link is approved in Google Ads, conversion uploads have nowhere to land. If deliveries fail on a brand new Google Ads destination, check the link request first.
        </Callout>

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an event to the destination" icon="git-branch">

    A destination on its own delivers nothing. At **Settings → Workflows → Connections**, drag from a source event to your destination, or click **New Connection**, then pick the event — **Made Any Purchase** for orders, **Opportunity Changed** for pipeline movement. The same panel carries filters and an event-name override.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify a delivery" icon="check-circle">

    Trigger the event in GoHighLevel, then open **Reporting → Workflows → Activity Log**: every processed event, newest first, with what was sent and what the destination replied. **Auto** refreshes it every 30 seconds.

    <Callout kind="success">
      A successful delivery is the finish line. From here it's Meta and Google's own reporting delay before the conversion shows up on their side.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>
