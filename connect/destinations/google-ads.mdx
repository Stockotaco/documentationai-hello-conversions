title: Google Ads
description: Link a Google Ads account to Hello Conversions and upload offline conversions — leads, purchases and pipeline stages — against the click that produced them.
---

Google Ads can only bid on conversions it knows about. A lead that closes a week later, an opportunity moved to Won, a purchase recorded in your CRM — none of that reaches Google on its own. A Google Ads destination uploads those conversions back to the click that produced them.

You don't paste any credentials. You enter the account's Customer ID, we send a manager account link request, and you approve it inside Google Ads.

<Callout kind="info">
  This is the Google Ads **destination** — conversions going out to Google. Pulling ad spend and campaign performance *in* for reporting is a separate connection: see [Google Ads as a source](/connect/sources/google-ads).
</Callout>

## Before you start

You need:

- The **Customer ID** of the Google Ads account, with or without dashes. It's at the top of the Google Ads dashboard, formatted like `123-456-7890`.
- Access to that Google Ads account with permission to accept a manager account request.

## Link the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the destination">
    In your project — one client account, or just your business if you're not an agency — go to **Settings → Integrations → Destinations**, click **Add Destination**, and choose **Google Ads**. Give it a name, enter the **Customer ID**, and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link request">
    The new destination shows as **Not Linked** with a **Send Link Request** button. Click it. Hello Conversions sends a manager account link request from its manager account to that Customer ID, and the status changes to **Pending Approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve it in Google Ads">
    Sign in to the Google Ads account and accept the pending manager account request. Nothing else in Google Ads needs configuring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to flip to Connected">
    Hello Conversions re-checks pending links about once a minute. When Google reports the link as active, the destination turns **Connected**, two conversion actions are created for you, and every owner on the organization gets an email confirming it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">
  The manager link belongs to the Customer ID, not to the project. If two projects have a Google Ads destination pointing at the same Customer ID, approving or removing the link in Google Ads affects both.
</Callout>

## Conversion actions

When the link is approved, Hello Conversions creates two conversion actions in the Google Ads account. Both are offline click-upload actions (`UPLOAD_CLICKS`), set to the account's own currency:

| Conversion action | Category | Counting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Purchases (Hello Conversions Server-Side)` | Purchase | Every conversion |
| `New Contacts (Hello Conversions Server-side)` | Sign-up | One conversion |

Both are created with a 30-day click-through window, a 1-day view-through window, and a data-driven attribution model. Neither is marked as a primary goal, so they don't change bidding on their own until you make them primary in Google Ads.

### Choosing which action an event uploads to

Each connection to a Google Ads destination gets its own **Conversion action** step (it replaces the custom event name step other destinations have — Google keys off the conversion action, not an event name). Open **Settings → Workflows → Connections**, edit or create a connection to the Google Ads destination, and pick an action from the dropdown. The list is fetched live from the account and shows every enabled conversion action, not just ours.

You can also create a new one from that dropdown without leaving Hello Conversions. You choose the name, category, counting type, whether it counts as a primary goal, and how value is handled:

| Value option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Dynamic** | Uses the value we upload with each conversion. |
| **Static** | Always uses the default value you set, ignoring the uploaded value. |
| **None** | Uses a default value of zero. |

If a connection has no conversion action set, uploads fall back to a default: `contact_created` events go to the New Contacts action, everything else goes to the Purchases action.

## What gets uploaded

Each delivered event becomes one offline click conversion upload containing:

| Field | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Click ID | `gclid` from the event, or `gbraid` / `wbraid` for iOS app clicks |
| Conversion action | The connection's action, or the fallback above |
| Conversion time | The event's timestamp |
| Value and currency | The event's value. Currency is the transaction's own currency, then the linked account's currency, then USD |
| Order ID | The order ID — or the transaction ID for subscription rebills, since rebills reuse the original order's ID |

The order ID is what stops double-counting: if the same sale is also counted by a tag on your site, Google collapses the two.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **An event with no Google click ID cannot be uploaded.** Google's offline conversion import requires a `gclid`, `gbraid` or `wbraid`. An event that has none — a lead who came from Meta, a pipeline stage change on a contact with no ad click, a walk-in — is skipped before the request is made and logged in the Activity Log as `filtered`, with the reason "No usable identifier for ingestion". This is expected behavior, not an error. Only the share of your CRM events that started with a Google ad click can be sent.
</Callout>

## Confirm it's working

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the destination status">
    On **Settings → Integrations → Destinations**, the card shows the link status: **Connected**, **Pending Approval**, **Access Revoked** or **Not Linked**. Use **Test Connection** on the edit panel to confirm the link is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Activity Log">
    **Reporting → Workflows → Activity Log** shows each event with its status and the exact payload we sent. `delivered` means Google accepted the upload; `filtered` usually means no click ID. See [Activity history](/conversion-tracking/activity-history).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Google Ads">
    Open the conversion action in Google Ads and look at its recorded conversions. Offline conversion imports are not instant in reporting — give it time before concluding nothing arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
  Google reports an upload as accepted at the moment of the request. Accepted is not the same as attributed: if the click ID is older than the conversion action's 30-day click-through window, or belongs to a different account, Google accepts the upload and then doesn't count it.
</Callout>

## If the link is removed

Hello Conversions re-checks connected accounts and notices when a manager link is removed or access is revoked. When that happens it:

- flips the destination to **Access Revoked**,
- disables every connection pointing at that destination, so nothing is silently dropped in the background,
- emails every owner on the organization.

To recover, click **Re-send Link** on the destination card, approve the request in Google Ads again, then re-enable the connections you want running under **Settings → Workflows → Connections**.
