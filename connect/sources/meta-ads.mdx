title: Meta Ads
description: Connect a Meta ad account as a source so campaign, creative, and spend data feeds the Creatives and Marketing Efficiency reports.
---

Connecting Meta Ads pulls your campaigns, ads, creatives and daily spend into Hello Conversions so cost sits next to the revenue you're already tracking. Each ad account is connected to the **project** it belongs to.

<Callout kind="warning">
  This is the **source** side of Meta: reading spend and campaign data *out* of your ad account. It is not the Meta Conversions API destination, which sends conversions *into* your pixel. Connecting one does not connect the other — see [Sources vs Destinations](/connect/sources-vs-destinations) and [Meta Conversions API](/connect/destinations/meta-capi).
</Callout>

## What it lights up

| Report | What Meta Ads provides |
| --- | --- |
| Creatives — Overview, Frameworks, Tracking | The whole report. Ad creatives, thumbnails, video, and per-creative performance all come from this source. |
| Marketing Efficiency | Per-campaign daily spend, clicks, and impressions — the cost half of every efficiency number. |
| Lead LTV | Ad spend by day, so lifetime value can be read against what you paid to acquire it. |
| Sources page | A 14-day spend sparkline with the total and trend, right on the Meta Ads row. |

Without it, those reports still load — they just have no Meta spend in them.

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your ad account ID">
    In Meta Business Suite, open **Ad Account Settings**. The ID looks like `act_123456789`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations → Sources**. Meta Ads is under **Ad Platforms**. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account ID">
    Paste the ID into the dialog. For several ad accounts in one project, separate them with commas — `act_123456789, act_987654321`. The `act_` prefix is optional; it's stripped either way. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Meta">
    Approve access on Meta, then you're returned to the Sources page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A "Meta Ads Connected" toast confirms the account is linked and an initial sync has been kicked off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How often it syncs

An initial sync runs on connect. After that the account syncs **daily at 02:00 UTC**.

- **Insights use a 28-day lookback.** Each run re-pulls the last 28 days of performance data, so Meta's late attribution corrections show up instead of freezing at day-one numbers.
- **Only active, paused, and in-process campaigns sync.** Archived and deleted campaigns are not pulled, so historical spend on an archived campaign won't appear.

## Telling it's working

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Green **Live** badge with a connection date | Connected and syncing. |
| Sparkline with a dollar total and a 14-day trend | Spend data has landed and the reports have something to read. |
| "Awaiting first sync" | Connected, but the first sync hasn't finished yet. Give it until the next daily run. |
| "Sync failed" | The last sync errored. Disconnect and reconnect; if it persists, contact support. |
| Amber **Reconnect** badge | Access was revoked on the Meta side. Reconnect to resume. |

<Callout kind="tip">
  A brand-new connection typically shows "Awaiting first sync" for a while, and that sync has to finish before the Creatives reports have anything to show. Don't judge the connection in the first ten minutes.
</Callout>

## Set the primary objective

Once connected, an **Objective** dropdown appears on the Meta Ads row. It tells the Creatives reports which Meta result to treat as the headline conversion.

The options are Purchases, Leads, Registrations, Add to Carts, Checkouts, Content Views, Contacts, Subscriptions, and Trial Starts. It defaults to **Purchases**. Changes save immediately, with a small "Saved" marker.

Pick what the account is actually optimizing for — a lead-gen client set to Purchases will show a Creatives report full of zeros.

## What gets pulled

| Data | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Campaigns | Campaign names and IDs for the efficiency reports |
| Ads | Ad-level structure underneath each campaign |
| Ad insights (by action type) | Spend, clicks, impressions, and results by day |
| Ad creatives | Creative copy, headlines, and configuration |
| Images and videos | Creative thumbnails and video assets shown in the Creatives reports |

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on the Meta Ads row stops the sync and removes the connection, after a confirmation. Reports that depend on Meta spend go quiet and synced data stops updating. Reconnecting means entering the ad account ID again.
