title: Meta Ads
description: Connect a Meta ad account as a source so campaign, creative, and spend data feeds the Creatives and Marketing Efficiency reports.

Connecting Meta Ads pulls your campaigns, ads, creatives, and daily spend into Hello Conversions so cost sits next to the revenue you're already tracking. It's per **project** — one client account, or just your business if you're not an agency — so each ad account gets connected to the project it belongs to.

<Callout kind="warning">
  This is the **source** side of Meta: reading spend and campaign data *out* of your ad account. It is not the same as the Meta Conversions API destination, which sends conversions *into* your pixel. Connecting one does not connect the other — see [Sources vs Destinations](/connect/sources-vs-destinations) and [Meta Conversions API](/connect/destinations/meta-capi).
</Callout>

## What it lights up

| Report | What Meta Ads provides |
| --- | --- |
| Creatives — Overview, Frameworks, Tracking | The whole report. Ad creatives, thumbnails, video, and per-creative performance all come from this source. |
| Marketing Efficiency | Per-campaign daily spend, clicks, and impressions — the cost half of every efficiency number. |
| Lead LTV | Ad spend by day, so lifetime value can be read against what you paid to acquire it. |
| Sources page | A 14-day spend sparkline with the total and trend, right on the Meta Ads row. |

Without it, those reports still load — they just have no Meta spend in them.

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your ad account ID">
    In Meta Business Suite, open **Ad Account Settings**. The ID looks like `act_123456789`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In Hello Conversions, go to **Settings → Integrations → Sources**. Meta Ads is under **Ad Platforms**. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account ID">
    Paste the ID into the dialog. To pull several ad accounts into one project, separate them with commas — `act_123456789, act_987654321`. The `act_` prefix is optional; it's stripped either way. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Meta">
    You're sent to Meta to approve access, then returned to the Sources page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A "Meta Ads Connected" toast confirms the account is linked and an initial sync has already been kicked off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How often it syncs

An initial sync runs the moment you connect. After that the account syncs **daily at 02:00 UTC**.

Two things worth knowing about what lands:

- **Insights use a 28-day lookback.** Each run re-pulls the last 28 days of performance data, so Meta's late attribution corrections show up in your reports instead of freezing at whatever the number was on day one.
- **Only active, paused, and in-process campaigns sync.** Archived and deleted campaigns are not pulled, so historical spend on a campaign you archived won't appear.

## Telling it's working

The Meta Ads row on the Sources page is the status readout:

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Green **Live** badge with a connection date | Connected and syncing. |
| Sparkline with a dollar total and a 14-day trend | Spend data has landed and the reports have something to read. |
| "Awaiting first sync" | Connected, but the first sync hasn't finished yet. Give it until the next daily run. |
| "Sync failed" | The last sync errored. Disconnect and reconnect; if it persists, contact support. |
| Amber **Reconnect** badge | Access was revoked on the Meta side. Reconnect to resume. |

<Callout kind="tip">
  A brand-new connection typically shows "Awaiting first sync" for a while, and the first sync has to finish before the Creatives reports have anything to show. Don't judge the connection in the first ten minutes.
</Callout>

## Set the primary objective

Once Meta Ads is connected, an **Objective** dropdown appears on its row. This tells the Creatives reports which Meta result to treat as the headline conversion — the one performance is judged on.

The options are Purchases, Leads, Registrations, Add to Carts, Checkouts, Content Views, Contacts, Subscriptions, and Trial Starts. It defaults to **Purchases**. Changes save immediately and a small "Saved" marker confirms it.

Pick whatever the account is actually optimizing for. A lead-gen client set to Purchases will show a Creatives report full of zeros.

## What gets pulled

| Data | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Campaigns | Campaign names and IDs for the efficiency reports |
| Ads | Ad-level structure underneath each campaign |
| Ad insights (by action type) | Spend, clicks, impressions, and results by day |
| Ad creatives | Creative copy, headlines, and configuration |
| Images and videos | Creative thumbnails and video assets shown in the Creatives reports |

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on the Meta Ads row stops the sync and removes the connection to your ad account. You'll be asked to confirm. Reports that depend on Meta spend go quiet, and data already synced stops updating. You can reconnect at any time — you'll be asked for the ad account ID again.
