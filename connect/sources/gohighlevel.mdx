GoHighLevel is where your contacts, opportunities and purchases live, so it's the source everything else hangs off. One project connects to one GoHighLevel sub-account (location).

<Callout kind="note">
  The app calls this integration **Lead Connector** — that's the name you'll see on the Sources page, in toasts, and in the sidebar. It's the same thing as GoHighLevel.
</Callout>

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sources page">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations → Sources**. Lead Connector sits at the top, under **CRM & Revenue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're sent to GoHighLevel to authorize Hello Conversions against the sub-account you want to track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the install">
    Approve the access GoHighLevel shows you and you're redirected back, where setup finishes on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it took">
    A "Lead Connector Connected" toast appears, and the row shows a green **Live** badge, the date it connected, and your contact count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">
  Finish the authorization in the same browser tab you started it in. Hello Conversions holds the project you're connecting in that tab's session — if it's lost, you'll see "Session expired. Please try connecting again from your project settings." Nothing is broken; just start over from the Sources page.
</Callout>

### Installing from the GoHighLevel marketplace

Installing from inside GoHighLevel sends you to a **Connect this Lead Connector location** screen, which asks which project the location belongs to. If it's already connected to a project, you go straight to that project's Sources page.

## What connecting sets up

- **Sets your project timezone** from the sub-account's — but only if the project is still on the default UTC. A timezone you set yourself is kept.
- **Provisions the reporting views** behind the Revenue, Lead LTV, and Marketing Efficiency reports.
- **Pulls your live contact count**, shown on the Sources row and used for your plan tier.
- **Carries over existing settings** if this location was set up on the older version of Hello Conversions — connected destinations and pipeline stage configuration, but only if the project doesn't already have its own.

## What syncs

### Scheduled data sync

Records synced on a schedule into your project's reporting warehouse. These are what the reports read:

| Data | What it's used for |
| --- | --- |
| Contacts | Contact counts, lead attribution, customer identity across reports |
| Opportunities | Pipeline reporting and opportunity-based conversion events |
| Transactions | Revenue reporting — the Revenue Overview and Lead LTV reports |
| Form submissions | Form-level reporting in Revenue Overview |
| Funnels | Funnel and page attribution |
| Products | Product-level revenue breakdowns |
| Pipelines | Pipeline and stage names used for configuring conversion events |
| Users | Attribution of records to the user who owns them |

### Real-time events

Lead Connector also sends events as they happen — a tag added, an opportunity created or moved, an order completed, a contact created. These drive conversion delivery: matched against your connections and forwarded to your destinations within seconds, not at the next scheduled sync.

<Callout kind="info">
  The full list of events, what each one fires on, and how they map to conversions is in [Event Types](/conversion-tracking/event-types).
</Callout>

## When the connection drops

If the app is uninstalled or authorization is revoked on the GoHighLevel side, the Sources row turns amber with a **Reconnect** badge and "Disconnected externally", plus a "Lead Connector Connection Lost" warning next time you open the page. Click **Reconnect** and authorize again. Synced data stays, but nothing new arrives while the connection is down, so conversions won't be delivered in that window.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on the Lead Connector row stops the sync and revokes the connection, after a confirmation. You can reconnect at any time.

Sync schedules, webhook delivery, and re-running a sync are handled by Hello Conversions on your behalf — if you need a sync re-run or a webhook checked, that's a support request.
