---
title: Google Ads
description: Connect a Google Ads account as a source so campaign and cost data feeds the Marketing Efficiency and Lead LTV reports.
---

Connecting Google Ads pulls your campaigns and daily cost into Hello Conversions, so Google spend lands alongside Meta spend and the revenue you're already tracking. It's per **project**.

<Callout kind="warning">
  This is the **source** side of Google Ads: reading cost and campaign data *out* of your account. It is not the Google Ads destination, which sends offline conversions *into* a conversion action. Connecting one does not connect the other — see [Sources vs Destinations](/connect/sources-vs-destinations) and [Google Ads Conversions](/connect/destinations/google-ads).
</Callout>

## What it lights up

| Report | What Google Ads provides |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing Efficiency | Per-campaign daily cost, clicks, and impressions — the cost half of every efficiency number for Google. |
| Lead LTV | Daily Google spend, broken out by advertising channel type, so lifetime value can be read against acquisition cost. |
| Sources page | A 14-day spend sparkline with the total and trend, on the Google Ads row. |

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your customer ID">
    In Google Ads, click the help icon in the top right. The ID looks like `123-456-7890`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations → Sources**. Google Ads is under **Ad Platforms**. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the customer ID">
    Paste it in with or without dashes — `123-456-7890` and `1234567890` both work. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Google">
    Approve access on Google, then you're returned to the Sources page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A "Google Ads Connected" toast confirms the account is linked and an initial sync has been kicked off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Sign in to the Google authorization step with an account that already has access to that customer ID. Authorizing with the wrong Google login is the most common reason a connection completes but no data ever arrives.
</Callout>

## How often it syncs

An initial sync runs on connect. After that the account syncs **daily at 02:00 UTC**, so cost data in the reports is up to a day behind — expected, not a fault.

## Telling it's working

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Green **Live** badge with a connection date | Connected and syncing. |
| Sparkline with a dollar total and a 14-day trend | Cost data has landed and the reports have something to read. |
| "Awaiting first sync" | Connected, but the first sync hasn't finished yet. |
| "Sync failed" | The last sync errored. Disconnect and reconnect; if it persists, contact support. |
| Amber **Reconnect** badge | Access was revoked on the Google side. Reconnect to resume. |

The other confirmation is Marketing Efficiency: once the first sync completes, Google campaigns appear there with cost against them.

## What gets pulled

| Data | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Campaigns | Campaign names, IDs, channel type, and daily cost, clicks, and impressions |
| Account performance | Account-level performance over time |
| Customer | Account details for the connected customer ID |
| Conversion actions | Conversions and conversion value by conversion action name and day |

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on the Google Ads row stops the sync and removes the connection, after a confirmation. Google cost disappears from new report runs and synced data stops updating. Reconnecting means entering the customer ID again.
