---
title: Sources vs Destinations
description: A source is a system Hello Conversions reads data from; a destination is where it sends conversions to — and Meta Ads and Google Ads are both.
---

Under **Settings → Integrations** there are two lists, **Sources** and **Destinations**. They share provider logos, which is why people connect one and expect the other to work. They are separate setups with separate credentials, and both are scoped to a **project** — connecting Meta Ads for one client does nothing for the next.

| | Sources | Destinations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Data flows **in** to Hello Conversions | Conversions flow **out** to the ad platform |
| Purpose | Reporting: spend, campaigns, creatives, revenue, contacts | Optimization: telling the ad platform a conversion happened |
| Where | Settings → Integrations → Sources | Settings → Integrations → Destinations |
| Examples | Lead Connector, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Stripe | Meta Conversions API, Google Ads |

## Meta Ads and Google Ads are both

<Callout kind="warning">
  Meta Ads and Google Ads appear in **both** lists, and connecting one does not connect the other. If ad spend shows in your reports but your Meta pixel isn't receiving conversions, you connected the source and skipped the destination — or the other way around.
</Callout>

They don't even use the same credentials:

- **Meta Ads as a source** — ad account ID plus Meta authorization. Pulls campaigns, ads, creatives, and spend.
- **Meta as a destination** — Pixel ID and a Conversions API access token. Pushes conversion events.
- **Google Ads as a source** — customer ID plus Google authorization. Pulls campaigns and cost data.
- **Google Ads as a destination** — delivers offline conversions against a conversion action in your account.

For the full picture on a client — spend on one side, conversions on the other — you need both.

## Sources that are only sources

**Lead Connector** (GoHighLevel) and **Stripe** are read-only. Lead Connector is where contacts, opportunities and purchases come from; Stripe is where charges come from. Nothing is sent back to either.
