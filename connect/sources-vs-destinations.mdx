---
title: Sources vs Destinations
description: A source is a system Hello Conversions reads data from; a destination is where it sends conversions to — and Meta Ads and Google Ads are both.
---

Under **Settings → Integrations** you'll find two lists: **Sources** and **Destinations**. They look similar and they share provider logos, which is why people connect one and expect the other to work. They don't. They are separate setups with separate credentials.

Both lists are scoped to a **project** — one client account, or just your business if you're not an agency. Connecting Meta Ads for one client does nothing for the next one.

## The difference

| | Sources | Destinations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Data flows **in** to Hello Conversions | Conversions flow **out** to the ad platform |
| Purpose | Reporting: spend, campaigns, creatives, revenue, contacts | Optimization: telling the ad platform a conversion happened |
| Where | Settings → Integrations → Sources | Settings → Integrations → Destinations |
| Examples | Lead Connector, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Stripe | Meta Conversions API, Google Ads |

A **source** is a system Hello Conversions reads data *from*. Reports are built on top of it — you connect Meta Ads as a source so ad spend shows up next to revenue, and you connect Stripe as a source so charges show up in the revenue reports.

A **destination** is where Hello Conversions *sends* conversions. When a lead comes in or a purchase lands in your CRM, the destination is what carries that event back to Meta or Google so their bidding algorithms can use it.

## Meta Ads and Google Ads are both

<Callout kind="warning">
  Meta Ads and Google Ads appear in **both** lists, and connecting one does not connect the other. If ad spend shows in your reports but your Meta pixel isn't receiving conversions, you connected the source and skipped the destination — or the other way around.
</Callout>

The two setups don't even use the same credentials:

- **Meta Ads as a source** — you enter your ad account ID and authorize Meta. This pulls campaigns, ads, creatives, and spend.
- **Meta as a destination** — you enter a Pixel ID and a Conversions API access token. This pushes conversion events.
- **Google Ads as a source** — you enter your customer ID and authorize Google. This pulls campaigns and cost data.
- **Google Ads as a destination** — this delivers offline conversions against a conversion action in your account.

If you want the full picture for a client — spend on one side, conversions on the other — you need both.

## Sources that are only sources

**Lead Connector** (GoHighLevel) and **Stripe** are read-only sources. Lead Connector is where contacts, opportunities, and purchases come from; Stripe is where charges come from. Nothing is sent back to either one.
