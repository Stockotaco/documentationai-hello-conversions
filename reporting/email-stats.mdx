**Reporting → Email → Email Stats** covers **all** email sent from a project's GoHighLevel location — workflow emails, campaign blasts and one-off sends alike. It needs the [GoHighLevel source](/connect/sources/gohighlevel) connected.

Events stream in live rather than through a nightly sync, cached for five minutes. The range covers **through today**, with presets of Last 7 / 30 / 90 / 180 Days, defaulting to 30, written into the page URL for **Copy link**. Every KPI card carries a trend badge against the **previous period**: the same number of days, ending the day before your range starts.

## The eight KPIs

| Card | Value | Rate shown against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | Messages the sending platform accepted for delivery | — |
| **Delivered** | Messages the recipient's mail server accepted | Accepted |
| **Open Rate** | Unique recipients who opened | Delivered |
| **Click-Through Rate** | Unique recipients who clicked | Delivered |
| **Hard Bounce** | Permanent failures — invalid address, domain doesn't exist | Accepted |
| **Soft Bounce** | Temporary failures — mailbox full, server down | Accepted |
| **Complained** | Recipients who marked it as spam | Delivered |
| **Unsubscribed** | Recipients who unsubscribed | Delivered |

Two derivations decide most disputes. **Opens and clicks count unique recipients, not events** — Apple Mail Privacy Protection fires repeated opens, and raw events would push open rates past 100%. **Open rate and CTR are both divided by delivered, not by opens**; the clicks ÷ opens figure some ESPs call click-to-open rate is much higher.

Trend badges show a point difference on rate cards (`+3.2pp`), a relative change on count cards (`+18%`), and `new` where the previous period had no volume. Colouring follows common ESP guidance: open rate above 25% and CTR above 3% read as good; hard bounce amber above 1%, red above 2%; soft bounce amber above 2%, red above 5%; complaints amber above 0.05%, red above 0.1%.

## List Health

A 0–100 composite of four deliverability signals:

| Component | Weight | Full credit at | No credit at |
| --- | :---: | --- | --- |
| Delivery rate | 40% | 95% or better | 80% or worse |
| Hard bounce rate | 30% | 0% | 2% |
| Complaint rate | 20% | 0% | 0.1% |
| Unsubscribe rate | 10% | 0% | 0.5% |

| Score | Band |
| --- | --- |
| 85–100 | **Healthy** |
| 60–84 | **Watch** |
| Below 60 | **At Risk** |

Any component past its safe threshold — delivery below 90%, hard bounce above 2%, complaints above 0.1%, unsubscribes above 0.5% — is listed under the bar with its actual rate. The anchors are tighter than the point at which mailbox providers retaliate, so the gauge moves to Watch while there's still time to fix something.

## Top Movers

The biggest open rate and CTR changes versus the previous period, in percentage points, split into risers and droppers. Only emails with **at least 100 delivered in both periods** are eligible.

## Revenue Attribution

Four cards on a **7-day lookback**:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Delivered → Purchases** | People who received an email and purchased within the lookback |
| **Delivered → Revenue** | Revenue from those purchases |
| **Clicked → Purchases** | People who clicked a tracked link and then purchased within the lookback |
| **Clicked → Revenue** | Revenue from those purchases |

<Callout kind="alert">
  These are **overlapping**, not additive. Anyone who clicked also received the email, so a purchase can be counted in both figures. Never add the two together and present the total as email revenue.
</Callout>

## The breakdown tables

Three tables by origin: **Workflows**, **Campaigns**, and **Other**. Each row is one email, with its latest event date, delivered count, failed / hard bounce / soft bounce / complained / unsubscribed counts each with their rate, and open rate and CTR. Workflows and Campaigns also get **Delivered $** and **Clicked $** columns; **Other** has no attribution columns, because attribution is only produced for workflow and campaign email. Every table has search, sortable columns, paging and CSV export.

Numbers not matching? [A report number looks wrong](/help-center/troubleshooting/report-numbers) lists the causes symptom by symptom.
