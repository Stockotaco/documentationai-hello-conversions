**Reporting → Revenue → Overview** (headed *Revenue Operations* in the app) is the project's ledger view: how much money came in over this window, from whom, and how people reached us. No attribution model, no channel split, no ad spend — that's [Marketing Efficiency](/reporting/marketing-efficiency).

## Where the numbers come from

Every figure is derived from a daily roll-up of the project's revenue source:

| Project has | Revenue is built from |
| --- | --- |
| **GoHighLevel only** | GoHighLevel transactions, net of refunds |
| **Stripe only** | Stripe charges, net of refunds, deduplicated |
| **Both** | Stripe for the charges Stripe processed, matched to the GoHighLevel transaction that shares its payment intent; GoHighLevel for everything Stripe never saw — cash, other processors |

Only live, settled money counts: GoHighLevel transactions at status `succeeded` or `refunded`, Stripe charges at `succeeded` and `livemode`. Test-mode transactions never appear.

| Panel | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Form Contacts, Form Submissions, Top Forms, Form Submission Pages | GoHighLevel form submissions |
| Page views, unique visitors, conversion rate | The [tracking script](/help-center/guides/install-tracking-script) on your pages |
| Top Products | GoHighLevel transactions, grouped by product name |
| Customer List | The revenue source, one row per customer |

## The KPI tiles

<Callout kind="info">
  Every tile compares against a window of the same length ending the day before your start date, and shows the percentage change. Twelve days selected means twelve days compared.
</Callout>

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **New Customers** (hero) | People whose first-ever purchase fell inside this window. |
| **Total Revenue Collected** (hero) | All settled revenue in the window, net of refunds. |
| **Form Contacts** | Distinct contacts who submitted a form in the window. |
| **New Customers — Single** | Same figure as the hero tile. |
| **New Customer Rev — Single** | Revenue from transactions that landed on the buyer's first-ever purchase day. |
| **Existing Customers — Single** | Distinct customers who bought in the window but had bought before. |
| **Existing Customer Rev — Single** | Revenue from everyone else's transactions. |
| **Total Transactions** | Count of settled transactions in the window. |
| **Avg Order Value** | See the warning below — it is not total revenue ÷ total transactions. |
| **Form Submissions** | Count of form submissions — one contact submitting three forms is three. |
| **Conversion Rate** | Transactions against unique visitors, from the tracking script. |

**New vs. existing** is decided against a customer's **first purchase ever**, not their first purchase in your window. Someone who bought in 2023 and bought again today is an existing customer, whatever date range you pick.

<Callout kind="warning">
  **Avg Order Value and Conversion Rate are averages of daily figures, not the ratio of the totals.** Each day gets its own AOV and the tile shows the mean, so a quiet Sunday with one $20 order pulls as hard as a Monday with two hundred orders. A client computing AOV by hand from Total Revenue ÷ Total Transactions gets a different figure, and their arithmetic matches the definition in their head. When a number has to survive a client conversation, take the ratio from the two totals yourself.
</Callout>

## The panels

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue chart** | New and existing revenue as stacked bars, form submissions as a line over the top. Follows the day / week / month toggle. |
| **Top Products** | GoHighLevel transactions by product name, with distinct customers and net revenue. GoHighLevel-only — Stripe charges don't carry a groupable product name. |
| **Top Forms** | Each form and the distinct contacts it captured, as a ranked bar. Contacts, not submissions — one person filling the same form twice is one contact. |
| **Form Submission Pages** | The same form data regrouped by originating page, with Submissions, Contacts, Customers and Revenue per page. Finds the landing page that actually produces buyers. Read its Revenue column with the caveat at the bottom of this page. |
| **Customer List** | One row per customer — Lead Date, First Purchase, # Purchases and LTV — with a click-through to that person's journey: when they were captured, which forms they submitted, and every purchase since. |

<Callout kind="warning">
  The Customer List uses your date range to decide **which customers are listed**, not what their figures say. A customer appears if they bought at least once in the window; their **# Purchases and LTV are lifetime totals**. A client narrowing to last week and seeing a customer with 14 purchases and $9,000 of LTV isn't looking at a bug — that's the customer's whole history, filtered to people active last week.
</Callout>

## Drilling into a number

Most tiles and table cells are clickable, opening the actual contacts or transactions behind the figure. If a client disputes New Customers, open the drill and read them the list.

Numbers not matching? [A report number looks wrong](/help-center/troubleshooting/report-numbers) lists the causes symptom by symptom.
